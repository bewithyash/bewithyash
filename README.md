### 👋 About

I break web, API, mobile and cloud systems to find where the real risk is, prove it with a working
proof of concept, and write it up. I run original vulnerability research with responsible disclosure,
hunt bug bounties, and publish anonymized case studies from the field.

- 🎯 Depth over coverage: broken access control, authN/authZ, business logic, mobile, and memory-safety research
- 🧪 Proof, not promises: every finding ships with a reproducible PoC and a false-positive gate
- ✍️ I publish what I find
