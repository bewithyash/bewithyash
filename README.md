### 🏗️ Building Imperon Labs

I founded [Imperon Labs](https://imperonlabs.com) to build security technology informed by real-world offensive research and experience leading enterprise security programs.

One problem I keep coming back to is the gap between what security tools report and what an attacker can actually exploit. Our work explores:

- **AI-led offensive security** &nbsp;·&nbsp; intelligent automation and attacker-like reasoning applied to penetration testing
- **Threat intelligence** &nbsp;·&nbsp; turning fragmented signals into actionable security context
- **Attack surface and exposure management** &nbsp;·&nbsp; understanding exposure and prioritizing meaningful remediation

The ambition is to make security testing more continuous, intelligent, and grounded in real-world exploitability. Still building, experimenting, and learning.

### 🧭 Research meets real-world security

As Head of Product Security I work across security engineering, cloud-native architecture, AppSec, DevSecOps, offensive testing, and the translation of technical findings into business risk. My experience spans fintech, telecom, media, IoT, defence, and government environments.

I have helped build security programs from the ground up and worked on frameworks and assurance efforts including ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, and SAMA CSF. Finding a vulnerability is one challenge; preventing an entire class of vulnerabilities across an organization is another.
